# NeighborAct

**A hyperlocal mutual-aid board where neighbors post what they need and claim what they can give.**

NeighborAct is a mobile-first web app built for real neighborhoods. No algorithms, no ads, no surveillance theater. Just a board of open needs — groceries carried, streetlights reported, someone to walk with after dark — and the people nearby who step up.

## How it works

1. **Post a need.** Pick a category, describe what you need in 280 characters, set the urgency, say how many people it takes, and tag your zone. A guided 6-step flow keeps posts focused and safe.
2. **Neighbors see the board.** Open needs show up as cards — filterable by category, sorted by urgency. Everyone in the neighborhood sees the same board.
3. **Claim with a message.** Tap "I can help," pick a quick-reply template (or write your own), and you're connected. No personal details are shared until both sides opt in.
4. **Resolve and build history.** Completed needs move to a "Recently Resolved" feed — visible proof that the neighborhood works. Each person's help count grows over time, building lightweight reputation without scores or ratings.

## Features

**Five need categories**

- **Mutual Aid** — groceries, rides, moving help, tool lending
- **Infrastructure** — potholes, downed trees, broken streetlights (co-sign city reports together)
- **Accompaniment** — walk with me, check on my house
- **Events** — block parties, cleanups, skill swaps
- **Prep** — storm prep, supply sharing, evacuation coordination

**Urgency tiers** — "Now" pushes a notification, "This week" lands in the digest, "Whenever" sits on the board with no rush.

**Safety by design** — Posts are blocked from including physical descriptions of people or exact street addresses. Emergency and crisis numbers are one tap away, always. No exact locations are shared; neighbors coordinate a meeting point in private messages after claiming.

**Neighborhood dashboard** — member count, active needs, total needs fulfilled. Moderators are visible. Local non-emergency numbers are listed.

**Lightweight reputation** — no stars, no scores. Just a running count of how many times someone has helped and how long they've been a member.

## What makes this different

NeighborAct is not Nextdoor. It is not Ring. It is not a feed.

- **Needs, not posts.** Every item on the board is something concrete someone needs done. There's no general discussion, no venting, no photos of packages.
- **No surveillance.** The keyword filter actively prevents descriptions of people. There are no cameras, no "suspicious activity" reports, no alert maps. Safety is handled through accompaniment — walking together — not watching.
- **Zones, not addresses.** Location is approximate by design. You say "Near the park" or "North Elm Park," never a street number. Coordination happens in private.
- **Finite and closeable.** Needs expire. Resolved needs are celebrated, not buried. The board is meant to be empty sometimes — that's a sign the neighborhood is working.
- **Reputation without rating.** You can see that Maria has helped 14 times and has been a member since January. You can't give her 3 stars. Trust is built through showing up, not through scores.

## Running locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no dependencies — it's a single self-contained HTML file.
